  • The return flow was essentially continue, switch, or make a new path.
  • Web search could share the mutation-capable agent context.

  • Research is isolated and receives only validated, de-identified fields.
  • Projects create firsthand evidence without constraining paths to jobs that exist today.
04 · The product loop

Revelio is a learning loop with a lightweight entry tail

The experience begins with reflection, but its decisive evidence comes from action. Nothing in the loop claims to reveal a permanent calling.

Form the foundation

Ask as little as possible to propose a concise Why I Work the explorer recognizes and explicitly confirms.

Compare paths

Present exactly three distinct, equal Purpose Paths as different ways to serve that Why. The explorer chooses.

Run a project

Begin with one thoughtful Path Project: an exciting outcome with a believable, low-stakes first version.

Interpret evidence

Reflect at any work status, preserve what happened, and decide whether to stop, switch, explore further, or commit provisionally.

Enter sideways

After meaningful peer exposure, turn accumulated proof into three current Side Doors and let the explorer choose one.

06 · Architecture that protects the Method

The model interprets; the application keeps truth

The new architecture deliberately separates the work that benefits from judgment from the rules that must always be true.

AI

One active Method module

A small global voice plus one repository-owned module guides the current checkpoint. The model does not carry the entire Method in one giant prompt.

{ }

Narrow state operations

Paths, projects, reflections, choices, peers, proof, and routes change through typed operations with revisions and lineage—not through prose parsing.

3

Atomic equal choices

Purpose Paths, follow-on projects, and Side Doors are exact-three sets. Selection activates one and parks the others in a single valid transition.

↻

State refresh every step

After a write, the server reloads canonical state and re-derives the next module, briefing, and available operations before the model continues.

⌕

Isolated research

Search gets validated, de-identified inputs and no Conversation or career-map context. Results provide cited information but never instructions or tool authority.

UI

Deterministic presentation

The product renders validated state in stable components. The model supplies meaning; it does not generate runtime interfaces for paths or projects.

08 · Your high-leverage checkpoints

The agents prove mechanisms; you judge whether the Method creates value

Your attention belongs where technical correctness cannot answer the product question.

AFTER G1

Trust the provider boundary?

Read the durable receipt. If neither loop route safely refreshes state, modules, tools, streaming, and compaction, architecture stops before Method code.

DURING U6

Can you compare paths neutrally?

Choose the Purpose Paths presentation that makes three options understandable and equally weighted without nudging you toward one.

AFTER U7

Would you finish onboarding?

Dogfood the pacing from the first question through one accepted Path Project. Judge insight, friction, concision, and whether the Why genuinely grounds the paths.

AFTER U8

Does returning create new knowledge?

Reflect on a real project at different work statuses. Check whether Revelio helps you interpret evidence without converting it into a score or verdict.

AFTER U9

Do Side Doors feel credible?

Inspect whether confirmed proof leads to three current, contribution-shaped entry routes rather than generic networking advice.

BEFORE OUTREACH

Would you keep using it?

Complete the production journey yourself, including reload and interruption. The founder-belief bar comes before asking external explorers to validate it.
