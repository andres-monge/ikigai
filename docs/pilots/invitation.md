# Early-access invitation

Draft to adapt for a specific participant before they agree. This file does not record that an invitation or notice has been delivered.

I'm inviting a small group to try Revelio while figuring out what work they want to explore. It helps you name a direction, consider different paths and learn through small projects. It is still an early product, and I want to understand what helps, what gets in the way and what you choose when the need comes up again.

**Before you agree:** I, the founder, and AI tools assisting me may read your full Revelio conversations to improve the product. This includes what you tell Revelio and its replies, rather than just anonymous usage counts. Please avoid including information you do not want reviewed, or private information about someone else. You can pause or stop participating and ask me about deleting your information.

Revelio uses Google identity for sign-in. Your saved Method/map information is stored in the application's database, and conversation/context is handled by OpenAI. The founder's private review notes and reports may include source-linked observations from these conversations. Ask the founder if you need more detail about storage, AI-assisted review or deletion before deciding.

You can use Revelio when a real question comes up; there is no obligation to keep opening it or to finish a project. We may also talk about what you were trying to do, what you used before and whether Revelio helped. I will tell you about any focused session or help I offer, so we can distinguish independent use from use with assistance.

If you would like to participate, reply to the invitation. Include the founder's actual reply-to contact when sending this draft; it is also the contact for questions and deletion requests. Record the notice version/source and agreement reference privately. Do not imply this draft establishes legal compliance or that the rest of the pilot erasure work is complete.
