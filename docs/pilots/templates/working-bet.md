# Working Bet

Fictional example: people considering a change of work may get more useful evidence from small firsthand projects than from choosing an identity in advance.

- Who has the job, and when does it come up?
- What do they do today, or what is newly possible?
- What do we think could be better?
- What is the biggest thing we need to learn?
- What will we do to find out?
