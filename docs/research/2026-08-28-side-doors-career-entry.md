# Side Doors as a Career-Entry Method

> Historical snapshot. This note records a practical source and its implications for Revelio as of 2026-08-28. It is research input, not current product authority or validated outcome evidence.

## Source

- Maja, ["How to Enter Side Doors"](https://velvetnoise.substack.com/p/how-to-enter-side-doors), *Velvet Noise*, published 2026-05-15; reviewed 2026-08-28.

## Core Idea

Conventional career entry treats a job as a posted role and the candidate's task as applying for it. The side-door frame starts one level deeper: organizations and individuals have problems they want solved, including needs that have not become job descriptions. A person can create an opportunity by making their interest, judgment, and usefulness legible to someone who recognizes the signal.

The source describes two complementary routes:

- **Outbound:** Find a specific person or organization doing work the explorer cares about, understand what they are trying to accomplish or where they may be stuck, and approach them with a relevant idea, question, artifact, introduction, or small piece of useful work.
- **Inbound:** Publish useful work so aligned people can discover how the explorer thinks and what they can do. Examples include tools, essays, analyses, prototypes, datasets, guides, videos, experiments, events, and communities.

Both routes rely on three qualities:

1. **Specificity:** Show real attention to this person, problem, or organization rather than sending a generic expression of interest.
2. **Proof:** Make it easy to see the explorer's thinking or ability through work, not only claims or credentials.
3. **Generosity:** Offer something useful without making the recipient responsible for inventing the opportunity or determining how the explorer might help.

The smallest useful proof is preferable to an oversized speculative project. The artifact should create enough signal to open a conversation, not attempt to solve an organization's entire problem without context.

## Relationship to the Revelio Method

Side doors belong after exploration has produced enough evidence for a provisional commitment. They are not a substitute for deciding what to pursue.

Revelio's Path Projects can create the raw material for career entry:

1. The explorer commits provisionally to one Purpose Path.
2. Revelio reviews the explorer's completed projects and identifies the clearest evidence of interest, judgment, and usefulness.
3. Revelio uses current web research to find people, communities, and organizations working on closely related problems.
4. The explorer chooses an outbound, inbound, or combined route.
5. Revelio helps turn existing work into a small, legible proof: for example, a focused prototype, teardown, memo, guide, analysis, or public project story.
6. Revelio helps the explorer make the approach specific by connecting the proof to a real person and problem.
7. The response becomes new evidence. A conversation, collaboration, role, or paid project is one possible outcome; silence or rejection still teaches the explorer about the terrain and can improve the next attempt.

This creates continuity between discovery and transition. The work used to learn whether a path fits is not discarded when the explorer commits; it becomes evidence that they have already started doing something useful in that area.

## Product Implications

- Do not reduce the commitment stage to resume rewriting, job-board matching, or application volume.
- Preserve Path Project artifacts and the context behind them so Revelio can later make the explorer's body of work legible.
- Search for people and unsolved problems, not only published job titles.
- Offer a small number of researched, high-fit starting points and explain why each is relevant.
- Support both outbound contribution and inbound public work; neither route should be mandatory in every case.
- Keep the explorer in control of external contact. Revelio may research, suggest, and draft, but should not send messages or publish work on the explorer's behalf.
- Treat specificity as a quality bar. Generic networking scripts and mass outreach contradict the method.
- Do not imply that audacity removes structural constraints. Access, privilege, networks, timing, financial runway, and luck still affect which doors exist and open.

## Evidence Limits

The source is a practitioner essay supported by personal stories and examples, not a comparative evaluation of career outcomes. Its examples demonstrate plausible mechanisms, not success rates or causal proof.

The method may work differently across occupations, geographies, seniority levels, regulated fields, and people with limited time or financial flexibility. Some careers still require formal credentials, licensing, structured recruitment, or standardized entry routes. Side doors should expand the explorer's available moves, not become a moral test of initiative or replace a suitable front-door application.

The strongest claim Revelio can make at this stage is that specific proof of work may create additional routes into a committed path. Whether Revelio can reliably help explorers identify and enter those routes remains a product hypothesis for later pilots.
