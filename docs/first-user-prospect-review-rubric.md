# First-user prospect review rubric

This is the canonical founder-calibrated rubric for reviewing potential Revelio
users after evidence collection and qualification. It governs future founder
reviews from 2026-09-13 onward.

It does not change the frozen collector or blinded-judge rules used by the
seven-day discovery trial. Those rules answer whether a public item contains
auditable evidence of unresolved work direction. This rubric answers the next
question: whether the person appears to be a good potential user, and how
strongly the public evidence supports inviting them into an early product test.

## The two decisions

Keep these decisions separate:

1. **Potential-user fit:** Does this person appear to have the problem Revelio
   is built to help with?
2. **First-wave suitability:** Does the public evidence suggest that this person
   is among the better candidates to try an unfinished, demanding early
   product?

A potential user can be postponed or ranked below the first wave without being
rejected. Audience size, outreach timing, and market sequencing are separate
from problem fit.

## Potential-user fit

A strong potential user has all three of these:

1. **Genuinely unresolved work direction or fit.** They have not settled what
   kind of work fits them. They may have several directions, a broad north star,
   or one serious hypothesis that still needs real-world testing. A precise
   destination with only application, salary, certification, visa, employer,
   or other execution questions is not enough.
2. **A substantial or persistent problem.** The uncertainty has meaningful
   consequences, has lasted, has survived a transition, or is materially
   affecting confidence, work, finances, or life. A near-term deadline is not
   required. Career discovery often takes time, and a long-running unresolved
   problem can be stronger evidence than momentary urgency.
3. **Openness to help or another approach.** They are asking for guidance,
   seeking a process, considering experiments, or otherwise showing that they
   have not closed themselves to learning what fits.

### Previous action is a bonus, not a gate

Courses, projects, jobs, conversations, research, applications, experiments,
and other attempts can show a bias for action and make someone more likely to
engage with Revelio. A person can still be a strong prospect without previous
attempts when the unresolved problem is substantial or persistent.

Do not merge persistence with previous action. Persistence describes how long
or deeply the problem has remained unresolved. Previous action describes what
the person has tried.

## Reading public evidence fairly

- Read the full post or comment, not only the retrieved sentence or title.
- When material, inspect relevant public follow-ups by the same author. A later
  post can show that the direction remains unresolved or that the person has
  since chosen a path.
- Treat missing evidence as unknown, not as evidence that the problem is weak.
- Base the review only on public, relevant work-direction evidence. Do not
  collect private contact details, infer sensitive traits, or inspect unrelated
  personal history.
- Emotional language can support substantial pain, but it cannot replace clear
  evidence that work direction or fit is unresolved.
- Do not call someone a proven desperate customer from a social post. Public
  evidence only identifies a promising person to test. Willingness to use a
  buggy product, complete projects, or pay must be learned through conversation
  and behavior.

## Important boundaries

### One named direction

A person considering one occupation can still qualify when they have not
decided that it fits and need experience to test the hypothesis. They do not
qualify when the occupation is effectively chosen and the remaining issue is
only tactical or external, such as applications, compensation, credentials,
visa mechanics, or a relationship constraint.

### Narrow choices inside an established path

Choosing among specializations, employers, or progression routes inside an
already chosen career normally falls outside Revelio's current problem. Retain
the person only when the evidence shows that the broader work fit is also open.

### Current status

If relevant later public evidence shows that the person has chosen and begun
executing a direction, mark the original prospect as no longer current. If a
follow-up merely describes an experiment and the person is already doubting
its fit, the underlying problem remains unresolved. When a follow-up is removed
or ambiguous, retain the original rating but flag current status for
verification before outreach.

### Initial market and universal-problem exception

The initial recruitment focus is the Anglosphere. Do not infer location. When
the public evidence explicitly places someone elsewhere:

- Exclude them from the initial pool when the decision materially depends on a
  local education, examination, government, government-employment, licensing,
  or labor-market system that could distort early product learning.
- They may remain in the initial pool as an explicit exception when the core
  work-fit problem is severe and portable, the possible solution does not rely
  on local institutions, and there is enough evidence that Revelio's method
  could apply. Record the exception rather than silently treating the market as
  equivalent.

## First-wave rating

Use a named rating plus a written reason. Do not reduce the evidence to one
numeric score.

- **Top-tier:** Exceptionally clear unresolved fit, substantial or persistent
  pain, and openness to a new approach. The evidence strongly suggests that the
  person may engage seriously with an early product.
- **Strong:** Clear potential-user fit and good first-wave evidence, but not as
  exceptional or complete as the top tier.
- **Promising:** Genuine problem fit, but public evidence leaves meaningful
  uncertainty about intensity, engagement, timing, current status, or initial
  market suitability.
- **Weak:** The person may benefit from Revelio, but the evidence suggests low
  first-wave value or only a peripheral version of the problem.
- **Exclude:** The direction is resolved, the request is tactical, the choice is
  too narrow, the evidence is not actually first-person and unresolved, or the
  case is outside the initial market without a justified exception.

Record **postpone** separately when the person fits but outreach should wait,
for example because they have a large audience or an ambiguous recent update.

## Review output

Present candidates in small batches. For each candidate, include:

1. Candidate number, public handle, and canonical evidence link.
2. A plain-English summary of the full relevant public context.
3. Brief rubric observations:
   - unresolved direction or fit;
   - substantial or persistent problem;
   - openness to help or another approach;
   - previous action as a bonus signal;
   - initial-market status or explicit exception;
   - current status when a relevant follow-up exists.
4. **My read:** one first-wave rating and the concrete reason for it.

Keep the presentation concise enough for the founder to answer with a simple
agreement or correction. When the founder corrects the reading, preserve the
reasoning behind the correction as calibration evidence rather than recording
only the final label.
