
## "Do Things That Don't Scale" First

We want to find the people who's hair is on fire and need this the most. What if the sequence was:

### Phase 1: Validate (Weeks 1-6)

**Goal:** Confirm the core value prop works before investing in SEO

- Find 30-50 target users through direct outreach:
    - r/careerguidance, r/findapath, r/cscareerquestions
    - LinkedIn (recently laid off, "open to work" folks)
    - Twitter/X (people tweeting about career crisis)
- Offer free access in exchange for a 15-min feedback call
- Watch them use it. Ask questions. Iterate weekly.

**What you learn:**

- Does the AI output resonate or feel generic?
- Is the action plan actually useful?
- What do they wish it did differently?
- Would they recommend it? Pay for it?

### Phase 2: Scale with SEO (Months 2-6)

**Goal:** Now that you know it works, build the traffic machine

- Commit to the domain/name based on what messaging resonated in Phase 1
- Build SEO content with confidence because you understand your user
- You'll write better, more specific content because you've talked to real people

---

## Why This Might Be Better for PMF

|SEO-First|Direct Outreach First|
|---|---|
|Passive, anonymous traffic|Active, conversational feedback|
|Learn slowly over months|Learn fast over weeks|
|Hope you built the right thing|Know you built the right thing|
|Commit to naming/positioning early|Test messaging before committing|
|Risk: rank for wrong audience|Risk: doesn't scale (yet)|

## The Uncomfortable Truth About PMF

Product-market fit isn't about traffic. It's about building something people **pull** from you rather than something you **push** to them.
 
Signs of real PMF:

- Users complete the full flow unprompted
- They download or copy their paths or action plan

You can't see these signals from SEO traffic alone. You need to talk to users.
