Teaching the people the system was built for

You stop translating complexity for one company and start making other people able to translate it for themselves.

The central unknown

Do you like teaching, or do you like being the person who knows?

What could become possible

Work that pays by the cohort rather than by the year, and material that keeps earning after you’ve stopped writing it.

Evidence it may fit

You’ve rewritten onboarding docs three times without being asked. The runbook you wrote in 2023 is still what people send to new hires.

First project

Run one 90-minute session for six people at a local nonprofit, on the tool they already hate. Charge nothing. Watch where they get stuck.

How it could fit your life

Independent instructors here bill €600–1,400 a day, but the first year is mostly unpaid audience-building. Evenings and one weekday would be enough to test it. No credential required; the buyers are ops managers, not L&D.

Building the narrow tool that deletes a step

Instead of explaining the complicated thing, you remove the part of it that never needed to exist.

The central unknown

Can you stay interested in someone else’s boring problem long enough to finish?

What could become possible

A product with maybe 200 customers that one person can maintain, in a trade you’d otherwise never have met.

Evidence it may fit

The scripts you wrote for your own team are the only side work you kept using after you stopped being paid to.

First project

Spend two weekends at one veterinary practice watching their intake process, then ship the ugliest possible version of the one thing that wastes the most time.

How it could fit your life

Vertical micro-SaaS typically reaches €2–5k monthly recurring in 18–30 months — well under your current income for at least two years. The only one of the three you can run without leaving your job. Distribution is the constraint, not code.

Working upstream, on how the systems get chosen

The complexity you keep absorbing was bought by someone in a meeting. You go to the meeting.

The central unknown

Can you tolerate a pace where a good outcome takes two years and has your name nowhere on it?

What could become possible

Influence over what an entire agency or hospital group makes people use, rather than over one codebase.

Evidence it may fit

You already do this informally. The vendor you argued against in 2024 was dropped, and you read procurement threads for fun.

First project

Write a public teardown of one live public-sector tender for a system you actually understand. Send it to three people who work in that space.

How it could fit your life

Civic and public-sector roles in your city pay €55–75k, a real cut from your current package. Entry usually runs through a fixed-term contract or a fellowship, and most people arrive from engineering rather than policy.
