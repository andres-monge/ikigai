### Your Ikigai Action Plan: The Next X Days/Months


## 1. Problem fit (Weeks 1-2, evenings)

1. **Interview 10 career-pivoters**
    
    - Target: adults 25-45 in Spain who changed jobs in the last 3 years.
        
    - Script: “What was hardest? What tools failed you? What would ‘magic wand’ fix?”
        
    - Log pain points in a spreadsheet and tag frequency.
        
2. **Competitor teardown**
    
    - Tools like Google’s _Career Dreamer_, LinkedIn’s _Next Role Explorer_, Salesforce’s _Career Connect_ already suggest paths via AI [wsj.com](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/ai-job-hunt-career-change-41f6bd4b?utm_source=chatgpt.com).
        
    - Note what they **don’t** do (e.g., deep values matching, Spanish-language coaching, one-click learning path).
        

Success checkpoint → At least 7/10 interviewees say current tools “still leave me stuck.” Otherwise, kill or pivot.

## 2. MVP v0.1 (Weeks 3-6, ~10 h/week)

|Feature|Keep it stupid-simple|
|---|---|
|**Intake chat**|One prompt funnel (“What makes you lose track of time?” etc.). Store as JSON.|
|**AI career matcher**|Use GPT function-calling: map interests + skills → 3 role buckets → give salary ranges from public APIs.|
|**Learning path stub**|For each role, list 5 high-signal resources (free Coursera, O’Reilly book, podcast). Hard-coded.|
|**Feedback widget**|Emoji + text field _inside_ chat—no email surveys.|

Budget: <€150/mo (OpenAI tokens + Supabase + Vercel).  
Stack: Next.js + shadcn/ui + TypeScript. You can copy/paste prompt prototypes from your Spotify Tutor experiments—just don’t import proprietary code.

## 3. Internal leverage play

|Step|What to do inside Spotify|
|---|---|
|**Pitch deck (1-pager)**|“Career Tutor: extend Learning Mountain from _skills training_ to _skills discovery_.” Stress that it converts lurkers into course buyers.|
|**Pilot ask**|10 % time allocation plus access to the Tutor team’s retrieval pipeline.|
|**Success metric**|Extra course purchases per 1 000 users who try career navigation vs. control.|

If accepted you get budget, brand, and zero IP headaches—worst case they say no and you stay indie.

## 4. User test & iterate (Weeks 7-8)

- Recruit first 30 testers (friends-of-friends from the interview pool).
    
- KPI: 25 % weekly retention on a _career-change task loop_ (come back, follow next learning bite).
    
- Add **“micro-quests”**: tiny reflection prompts to push them back to the app—borrow Duolingo’s streak psychology. Duolingo openly says AI is the future of scalable learning [campustechnology.com](https://campustechnology.com/articles/2025/05/06/duolingo-embraces-ai-in-push-for-scalable-learning.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com).
    

Green light → retention ≥25 % and at least 5 testers ask, “When is the next module?”  
Red light → retention <15 % → refine niche or kill.
